## Reconstruction implication

Proceed under **POLICY B — RECONSTRUCTED_EXPERIMENTAL_LIKE_FAB**:

- Append canonical human **IgG1 CH1** (+ minimal hinge stub through inter-heavy–light Cys for a chemically complete Fab model; terminus labeled uncertain).
- Append **Cκ (IGKC)** or **one predeclared Cλ (IGLC2)** by light-chain locus.
- Never claim exact experimental Fab AA sequence.
