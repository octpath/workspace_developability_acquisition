What is DOTAD?

DOTAD is an antibody developability database integrating metadata, sequence resources, experimental developability data, and literature-curated developability evidence into a unified antibody-centered platform.

It supports structured exploration, similarity/network analysis, release-centered download, and reusable data assets for benchmarking and computational method development.
