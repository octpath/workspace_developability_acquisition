Welcome to DOTAD!

DOTAD,a Database of Therapeutic Antibody Developability, is the first all-inclusive database of therapeutic antibody developability information available on the market, providing a platform for storing, browsing, and searching for therapeutic antibody items that are accessible for research and development. Users can access the DOTAD through http://i.uestc.edu.cn/DOTAD/.

As of December 2023, the DOTAD database contains approximately tens of thousands of sequences, of which around 1,000 have been approved for clinical trials or have received INN names. In terms of experimental physicochemical data measurements, the DOTAD database contains approximately 3,500 data points.
